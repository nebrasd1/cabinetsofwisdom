# Zotero Issues:
- Zotero highlights are done in its own database and not embedded on the pdf
	- This means that the links to the highlights within obsidian note of annotations links back to zotero app reader of the pdf
- Obsidian introduced deep links for pdfs, so if annotations are made directly/embedded on the pdf, they show up and you can copy links to the annotations that work within obsidian
	- I prefer this, because clicking on the annotation link opens up the pdf within obsidian, all staying in the app instead of going to another app
	- Limitation is that I don't have a process to automatically grab the obsidian annotation link and place it in the annotations note.. it has to be done manually.
* Readwise handles pdfs and annotations in same fashion as articles, but issue is that it opens up the link to web-based readwise pdf, and doesn't go directly to the highlight.
	* Not ideal because opening to the web and online instead of within the app and local
	* Readwise does have option to "download with annotations" which i could add to obsidian after i'm done annotating... this could be something?
