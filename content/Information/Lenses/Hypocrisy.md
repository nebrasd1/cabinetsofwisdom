# Hypocrisy

## Personal Definition

Humans are messy, and we have this ever-present gap between rhetoric and behavior. Do as I say, not as I do... and all that jazz. The opposite of authenticity. Stating one thing, claiming a certain value, and then just doing its antithesis.
