# Anarchy
## Personal Definition
Interested in this social system of organization without an imposed hierarchy, and all the trappings that come with it, such as mutual aid, volunteering, self-governance, being skeptical of authority (or is it non-consensual authority?).

This will come up when the topic is specifically political in nature and centered on the absence of authority. [[Community|Community]] deals with similar things like mutual aid, but that lens is regardless of issues of hierarchy or governance. Also contrast with [[Socialism|Socialism]] since that's a fellow political lens focused on aid social-first, but that one accepts having institutions.

## AI's version
The lens of organization without imposed hierarchy — mutual aid, voluntary association, self-governance, skepticism of coercive authority (state, corporate, patriarchal). 

Reach for this lens when the analytical move is _specifically political_: claiming that horizontality enables what hierarchy suppresses, or that the absence of imposed authority is itself the source of functional collective life.

Distinct from **Community** (descriptive — people sustaining each other, regardless of governance form) and from **Socialism** (which generally accepts collective institutions, state or worker-controlled; anarchy is skeptical of _all_ institutionalized coercive power). Use this lens to recover anarchy's actual meaning from common distortion: it's not chaos or violence, it's a serious political tradition centering autonomy and mutual aid.
