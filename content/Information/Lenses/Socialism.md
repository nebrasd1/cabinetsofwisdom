# Notes
- basic needs are met
	- you dont have to work to survive
- jobs can be distributed equitably rather than overwork some and maintaing unemployed rest that will work for the least pay
	- the incentive is maximize human potential, not shareholder profit
- your boss is accountable to you / there is no hierarchy above you
	- your boss is elected by you and your fellow workers, not by the shareholders
	- instead of the situation where your only recourse is to do what you're told, or threaten to leave your job
- In this kind of society, where you choose what to do, you make your own work, it's low hours, how do we get the shit jobs done as well?
	- we incentivize more innovation and automation to assist
	- we can incentivize low-desire jobs by paying more or providing more benefits
		- People like doing things that matter in conditions that fulfill their needs and desires.

Early stage communism: "From each according to their ability, to each according to their contribution"
Late stage communism: "From each according to their ability, to each according to their need"
