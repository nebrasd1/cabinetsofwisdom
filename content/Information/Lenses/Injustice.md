# Injustice

## Personal Definition

Moral indignity or violation. Actions and systems that break with what's fair or expected and ostensibly agreed upon. Denying what people have collectively agreed upon that they are owed (I get this is imprecise, so will need to fix it up). Power dynamics are an important factor, as are agreed upon values and what's collectively considered right or wrong.
