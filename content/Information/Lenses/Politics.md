# Politics

## Personal Definition

???
The lens of power and governance as they shape people's lives — how governments, institutions, and their proxies maneuver, control, oppress, or (more rarely worth noting) function in genuine service.

Reach for this lens when the analytical move is about _power affecting people through institutional or quasi-institutional means_, whether the analysis is critical (dark money, captured agencies, systems of oppression) or constructive (governance done well, collective public action, functional public servants).

The broadest political lens; the others (**Fascism**, **Anarchy**, **Socialism**) are specific positions or systems within the political dimension. Often co-occurs with **Capitalism**, **Propaganda**, **Injustice**, or **Hypocrisy** — that co-occurrence is informative, not redundant.
