## Description

Leader character bio
Survivor of rebellion of poorly batched clones that tore through this person's childhood center.. leaving friends and teachers dead and their face hideously scarred. Growing up disfigured is academic/professional suicide, so they searched for decades until they found risky pretech grafting sciences in an abandoned ancient medical ruin, and recreated their face perfectly. On surface they are healed and happy, but below the surface, the rejection and contempt and isolation has left their invisible scars and trauma.
