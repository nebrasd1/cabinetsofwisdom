# Overview
## Description
The idea of having rival factions on a world, both using the same tech, one for ostensibly "good" reasons, and the other more "selfish" reasons.. but still both operating under the mantle of trying to serve the greater good. 

The good hold themselves to moral standards and won't operate under the "ends justify the means"... the other faction have no qualms and will do whatever it takes to gain the edges, make the choices, etc. that serve the greater good.
Live a thief rather than die in honor kinda deal.
