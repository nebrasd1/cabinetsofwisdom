# Notes
## Setup
- add the additional crisis control cards to the factions
- don't use historical cards
- Add the imf cards
- Use the white events deck if Faisal is playing state again

## Teach
- Teach everyone the goals.. how all factions get points and win
	- Capitalist: collect money (thru production) and products
	- Working class: prosperity and trade unions.. by buying goods from others (education is key for both)
	- Middle: to be self-sufficient.. gain prosperity by buying goods (ideally from self), make lots of companies that are operational, collect money and products
	- State:  

## Strategies
Capitalist
- take loans, build companies early like crazy
- Trade your goods to foreign market for VPs
- FIRST.. low taxes, low wages
- SECOND.. high immigration, high tariffs

Middle
- build companies of each industry and get them producing quickly.. this will enable more prosperity by buying from yourself and then from others
	- Bonus if you keep more companies than prosperity
	- get your people working in your companies
- FIRST.. 7A low immigration
- SECOND.. you're happy at mid policies.. everyone else benefits more with the extremes
- do extra shifts always?

Working
- Trade unions right away (but how.. it takes forever to get 5 skilled workers of each industry.. you'd have to literally use every turn of every round to upskill workers in order to make this happen, ignoring the fact you still need food and money and good policies)
- increase prosperity always, by buying health/ed/lux
- FIRST Education (to get skilled workers... to get unions)
- SECOND high wages, low tariffs, low immigration

State
- do the events
- keep legitimacy up and balance the other players
- don't worry about agendas
- avoid IMF like the plague.. keep afloat
- FIRST.. High taxes, mid health and edu
- SECOND.. low/mid public sector, low wages
