# Tips

## Nationbuilding
- **Small/medium economies:** Prioritize Mission Control first, then shift to Boost and Funding once MC is where you need it
- **Always keep Economy and Knowledge running** — these compound over time and underpin everything else
- Unrest
	- Add **Welfare** when inequality is high (>3), **Unity** when cohesion is low (<5)
	- Keeping roughly 10–30% of priorities pointed at stability-oriented categories (Unity, Welfare, Knowledge in that order) helps keep nations from degrading over time.
	- Faction popularity and faction control matching up is better for cohesion.
- Prioritize Mission Control, then Boost once that's stable.
- **Spoils** — skip it. It degrades both your nation's economy and the global economy, and it's not worth the risk of triggering a coup.

## Other Missions to do early on
- **High Admin person does Hostile Takeovers**: Do some investigating and spamming Hostile Takeovers to get orgs, especially those that give admin.
- **High Persuasion person does (Control) and Public Campaigns:** Good for taking over places.
- **Purge:** If you want to kick them out of nations you care about.
- **High Military person does Crackdown/Stabilize:** use military councilors to keep your nations in check.
