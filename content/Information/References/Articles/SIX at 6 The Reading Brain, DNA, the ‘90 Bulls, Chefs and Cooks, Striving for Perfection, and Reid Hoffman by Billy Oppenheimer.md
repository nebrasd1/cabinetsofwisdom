### id518302195
> Dan Charnas points out that there’s a difference between striving for perfection and being a perfectionist. Their aims are the same—the highest quality—“but the striver knows that excellence is not about *creating* something of the highest quality,” Charnas writes, “it’s *delivering* something of the highest quality, with all the constraints that delivery entails—deadlines, expectations, contingencies, feedback.”
