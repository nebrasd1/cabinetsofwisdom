### id534189548
> In [other countries](https://www.peoplespolicyproject.org/2021/10/26/the-case-for-making-parental-leave-actually-universal/), parental leave programs are commonly designed such that all new parents receive a cash benefit equal to some percentage of their prior earnings or some minimum amount, whichever is greater in their case. But Minnesota’s plan has no minimum benefit and entirely excludes all new parents who did not earn $3,500 in the twelve months leading up to birth.
