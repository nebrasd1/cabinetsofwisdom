### id757170376

> Five tenants’ unions from around the country convened on Tuesday to announce the launch of a new national organization to take on the power of multistate real-estate capital. The Tenant Union Federation marks the first major national effort at tenant organizing in forty years.

### id757170377

> In the last year, the Louisville Tenants Union passed [far-reaching restrictions](https://www.lpm.org/news/2023-11-10/louisville-metro-council-passes-anti-displacement-ordinance-that-aims-to-combat-gentrification) on public funding contributing to gentrification in Louisville; KC Tenants [defeated](https://inthesetimes.com/article/kansas-city-stadium-tax-royals-chiefs-democracy) a billionaire-backed stadium tax in Kansas City; Bozeman Tenants United [banned new short-term rentals](https://inthesetimes.com/article/tenants-bozeman-union-developments-city-hall-homeless#:~:text=The%20strategy%20worked%3A%20In%20October,rentals%2C%20grandfathering%20in%20many%20others.) and elected one of their own as mayor in the Montana tourism hot spot; the Connecticut Tenants Union negotiated [a collective bargaining agreement](https://inthesetimes.com/article/tenants-union-mega-landlord-bargaining-table-new-haven-connecticut-blake-street-16-evictions-rent-hike) with one of New Haven’s largest landlords and Chicago’s Not Me We won ballot referenda backing [a landmark antidisplacement ordinance](https://southsideweekly.com/referenda-wins-behind-them-cba-organizers-fight-on/) covering the area surrounding the new Obama Presidential Center.
