> This is what mutual aid looks like—not a hashtag, not a buzzword, but real people choosing to care for one another when the system refuses to.

Because in this country, only [27 percent of workers have access to paid family leave](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R44835#:~:text=According%20to%20a%20national%20survey,supported%20short%2Dterm%20disability%20insurance), and [one in four mothers](https://www.vox.com/2015/8/21/9188343/maternity-leave-united-states) returns to work within just two weeks of giving birth. Because bed rest isn’t covered. Because healing isn’t honored, and rest isn’t protected.
