## Highlights

### id589599109

> Replacing asphalt with greenery has benefits beyond [lowering temperatures](https://www.epa.gov/heatislands/using-cool-pavements-reduce-heat-islands) and [reducing flood risk](https://www.epa.gov/soakuptherain/soak-rain-permeable-pavement). It’s also associated with [lower stress levels](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5663018/), [a reduction in noise](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1618866715001296), [fewer traffic-related injuries](https://depts.washington.edu/hhwb/Thm_SafeStreets.html#:~:text=One%20study%20found%20a%2046,after%20landscape%20improvements%20were%20installed.\&text=Another%20study%20found%20that%20placing,by%205%25%20to%2020%25.) and even [restoration of local biodiversity](https://sponsored.chronicle.com/can-cities-rejuvenate-biodiversity/index.html). It can also improve air quality: asphalt [releases hazardous air pollutants into communities](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abb9785), especially in extreme heat and direct sunlight.
