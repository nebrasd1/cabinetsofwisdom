### id813706478
> [half of the city’s residents](https://shelterforce.org/2023/06/30/social-housing-how-a-new-generation-of-activists-are-reinventing-housing/) live in social housing, which creates a price-dampening effect on for-profit housing that is forced to compete with high-quality subsidized housing. That competitive pressure combines with vigorous rent control on private housing to make Vienna one of the [cheapest renting cities](https://blog.datawrapper.de/how-vienna-found-a-unique-model-for-low-rent/) in all of Europe
