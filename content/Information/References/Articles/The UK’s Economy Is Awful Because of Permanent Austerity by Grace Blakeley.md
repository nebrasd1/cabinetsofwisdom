> If the UK economy is to transition to net zero, huge amounts of investment will be required in improving public transport infrastructure, expanding renewable energy generation, and improving home insulation. > What’s more, longer-term investments such as increasing funding for the research and development of green technologies could significantly improve productivity. And [research from Positive Money](https://positivemoney.org/publications/inflation-as-an-ecological-phenomenon/) has shown that reducing our dependence on fossil fuels would help to curtail inflation.
