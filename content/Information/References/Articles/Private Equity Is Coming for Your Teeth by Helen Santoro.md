> In the last decade, private equity firms have been quietly taking control of dental care from behind the scenes, largely through secondary business organizations that push dental practices to cut costs and, in some cases, encourage unnecessary and irreversible dental procedures.

### id917676806
> Private equity firms [usually aim](https://www.levernews.com/thanks-to-wall-street-there-goes-the-neighborhood/) to generate large profits over a short period of time by buying a business, cutting costs through measures like [layoffs](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3465723), and eventually selling it to other investors or sending the business into [bankruptcy](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2025/1/pe-backed-company-bankruptcies-in-us-reach-record-high-in-2024-87023731).
