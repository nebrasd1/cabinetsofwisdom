✏️ Things can be of a time, and can be used to describe things, or a method of how to do things, but something written a few hundred years ago is not necessarily going to be the answer to what ails us now.
 It guides us, but it's not the cure-all. It doesn't have to be, nor could it realistically be.
 Especially one that's written about a certain place, at a certain time, by a certain person with a certain lens. The world and humanity are both extremely complex and diverse things.
