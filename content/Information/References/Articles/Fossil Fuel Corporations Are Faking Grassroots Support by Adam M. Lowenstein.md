> “Energy Citizens,” an ultimately successful astroturf campaign launched by the American Petroleum Institute while Edelman was the organization’s single largest contractor, hewed closely to Edelman’s proprietary insights about trust. The effectiveness of Energy Citizens, which involved making oil and gas workers the “[human face](https://web.archive.org/web/20190308195551/https:/grist.org/article/2009-08-17-astroturf-wars-continue-api-energy-citizen-rallies/)” of the fossil fuel industry to create the impression of widespread grassroots support, [contributed](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/17524032.2023.2255388) to the [defeat](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/anatomy-of-a-senate-climate-bill-death/) of US climate legislation in 2010.
