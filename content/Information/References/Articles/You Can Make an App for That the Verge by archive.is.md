## Highlights
### id1017721943
> In this new world, the most important thing you’ll need is taste. Not objectively good taste, necessarily, so much as a keen sense of your own. You need to be like Rick Rubin, the famous music producer, who once [told *60 Minutes*](https://archive.is/o/vOMhT/https://www.cbsnews.com/news/rick-rubin-anderson-cooper-60-minutes-interview-2023-01-15/?ftag=CNM-00-10aab7d&linkId=197374877) that what made him successful was not any particular technical ability, but “the confidence I have in my taste, and my ability to express what I feel.” Rubin practices that art with A-list celebrities; you need to be able to do it with AI. Otherwise, you’ll land in what Lovin calls “doom loops,” telling your chatbot only what you don’t like and counting on the model to be the creative one. That way lies madness — and bad software.
