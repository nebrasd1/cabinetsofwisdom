### None
> Except, of course, speeders are only fast from the perspective of normal people. From their own point of view, they move at regular speeds. Tasks that those around them think are finished almost instantly are still time-consuming from the viewpoint of someone whose perceptions work a thousand times faster. Furthermore, from the speedster’s perspective, everyone around them moves at the speed of cold molasses. A five-minute conversation might to them feel like it consumed weeks.

✏️ I feel like this is highly underexplored, and potentially misused. We are always only seeing things from our perspective, but from the speedster's pov, he's doing things at normal speed; it's just everyone else is super slowed down.
 so it still takes him a few hours to days to read a book, it's just that he can do it without that actual time passing in real life. I mean, in that case, how would i ever stay focused for that long to read a whole book in "one moment"? I'm not exactly reading it at super speed; unless we factor in his fast thinking? Does that allow for faster absorption?
 Still, a task like cleaning up the house would still then take however many hours it would take right? He'd still feel that effort and mundanity the entire time?
