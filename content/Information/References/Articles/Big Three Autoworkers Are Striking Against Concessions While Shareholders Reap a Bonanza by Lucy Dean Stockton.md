### id596859706

> the Big Three automakers — General Motors, Ford, and Stellantis — have authorized $5 billion in stock buybacks, effectively giving billions of dollars to shareholders that could have gone to autoworkers.
> On top of the stock buybacks, the Big Three have reported [$21 billion]\(https://uaw.org/president-fains-big-three-contract-update-ford-proposal-insults-worth/) in profits in just the first six months of 2023. Despite the enormous gains, the companies have cried poverty in response to union demands for wage increases to make up for decades of pay stagnation.

✏️ The severe disconnect between corporate greed and just wages.
