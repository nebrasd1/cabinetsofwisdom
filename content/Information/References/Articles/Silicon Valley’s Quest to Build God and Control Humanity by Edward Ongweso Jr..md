### id576874218
> Like China, the United States uses AI for authoritarian ends at home and abroad. Surveillance and social control structure much of the digital technology we create. Global efforts to replicate our technological development have helped preserve the very regimes Andreessen claims must be opposed. From authoritarianism in the [Kingdom of Saudi Arabia](https://www.vox.com/technology/2023/5/1/23702451/silicon-valley-saudi-money-khashoggi) and the [other Arab states](https://www.ft.com/content/567ca518-b138-4273-bfe6-0712ef31e01d) of the Persian Gulf to [apartheid in Israel](https://www.middleeasteye.net/discover/palestine-laboratory-israel-antony-loewenstein-book-review) to [China’s totalitarianism](https://www.vox.com/recode/2019/5/1/18511540/silicon-valley-foreign-money-china-saudi-arabia-cfius-firrma-geopolitics-venture-capital), Silicon Valley tends to look the other way if money is to be made. Andreessen’s investment firm is [openly courting](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/01/andreessen-horowitz-is-now-openly-courting-capital-from-saudi-arabia-despite-u-s-strains/) Saudi Arabia for financing. So much for the rallying cry to stop authoritarian tech proliferation.
