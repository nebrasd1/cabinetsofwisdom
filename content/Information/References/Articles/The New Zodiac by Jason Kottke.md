Astrology as we know it, being the idea that we can see our fates in the stars.. is a bit suspicious if only because modern signs and horoscopes are talking about constellations that were in that particular bit of sky 2500 years ago.
The earth wobbles, the constellations have varying sizes, and there was a 13th sign/constellation that was dropped (perhaps for the sake of matching up with 12 months).. and so the constellations are not in the same spots anymore.
