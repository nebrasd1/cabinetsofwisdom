## Highlights

### id874463035

> Working in the open means that whatever I’m trying to do is far more likely to happen, or that my inchoate thoughts are more likely to be turned into action by somebody else.

✏️ Motivation that, no matter what you feel about what you're doing (especially if you think it's meaningless), it might be part of something bigger down the line. It'll inspire, it'll support, it'll make someone think a certain way.. it's never a bad thing.
