> The idea that free speech is saying whatever you want whenever you want [is wrong on a fundamental level](https://badfaithtimes.com/we-need-a-new-concept-of-free-speech-and-we-need-it-now/). This is what’s termed “reckless speech,” and it in no way should be protected by the First Amendment, as Mary Anne Franks argues in her excellent book, Fearless Speech. Free speech was meant to empower the powerless against the powerful, not to provide protection for society’s privileged members to hurl invective at the unprivileged and marginalized.
