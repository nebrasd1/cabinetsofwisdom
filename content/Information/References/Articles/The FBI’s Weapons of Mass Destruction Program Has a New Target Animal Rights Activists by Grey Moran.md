> The agent then gave a glimpse into the [legal strategy](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25207212-fbi_statutes) the FBI has been exploring, including potential charges under three federal criminal statutes that cover biological forms of WMD. One [statute](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2332a) defines a WMD as “any weapon involving a biological agent, toxin, or vector” and specifies that the damage inflicted by a WMD can conclude the “deterioration of food.” Another [statute](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/842) relies on the same definition for a WMD, but criminalizes sharing information about how to make or use these unconventional weapons.
