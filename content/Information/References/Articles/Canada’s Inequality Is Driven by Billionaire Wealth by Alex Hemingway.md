## Highlights
### id874443424
> the top 20 percent of Canadian households controls 64.7 percent of wealth, with an average CAD $3.3 million per household, while the bottom 40 percent holds only 3.3 percent of the total wealth with an average $83,189 per household.

🔗 [View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jra48hy0sqdjj3z79jbgvj75)

### id874443420
> The PBO’s wealth distribution [modeling](https://www.pbo-dpb.ca/en/publications/RP-2122-023-M--estimating-top-tail-family-wealth-distribution-in-canada-updates-trends--estimation-extremite-superieure-distribution-patrimoine-familial-canada-mises-jour-tendances) finds the richest 1 percent controls nearly a quarter of Canada’s wealth (24.3 percent), amounting to $3.5 trillion. Academic research using a similar modeling approach [finds](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/roiw.12453) that the wealth share of the top 1 percent may be even higher at 29 percent of net wealth. “Real-time billionaires” data from *[Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/real-time-billionaires/)* find that sixty-five Canadian billionaires now control approximately $410 billion of wealth. The Canadian Business [rich list](https://macleans.ca/society/canadas-richest-people/) found the richest ten billionaires alone controlled $261 billion.

### id874443274
> Tax policy can’t do all the heavy lifting, however. Blunting the power of the superrich and tackling inequality also require policies that help build working-class power, including making it easier to [unionize](https://jacobin.com/2023/04/british-columbia-card-check-unionization-organizing-surge) and fight for higher wages, to increase [working-class representation](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691182001/the-cash-ceiling) in government, and to enable workers to take charge in the workplace through [democratic employee ownership](https://dspace.library.uvic.ca/items/b54e8151-ccf3-4985-a2f2-8fb292bb6810) of firms.

✏️ A reminder that taxes can't fix everything. You still have to empower the working class, give them more wages, more power, more decision-making.
