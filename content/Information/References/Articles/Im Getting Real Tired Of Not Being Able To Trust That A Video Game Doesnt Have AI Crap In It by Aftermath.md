> oes it really matter if they slipped some AI stuff in there if I didn't notice it? Especially when, as developers 11 Bit [say](https://bsky.app/profile/11bitstudios.bsky.social/post/3lstiwaoxcc2i?ref=aftermath.site), it only constitutes "0.3%" of the overall text in the game?
> Yes, it fucking does. I am not just against the employment of this tech for obvious, superficial reasons. All generative AI content is built on theft; surely writing some gibberish, or pasting some lorem ipsum, would have been preferable here? And [saying there were "time constraints"](https://bsky.app/profile/11bitstudios.bsky.social/post/3lstiwaoxcc2i?ref=aftermath.site) involved in rushing AI-generated translations into the game is no excuse, either. Translators are highly skilled workers; using reputable ones costs a studio money, and takes time, but it avoids situations like _all_ _this_, not to mention it’s just the right and professional thing to do.

✏️ An argument for having zero tolerance about AI in video games. Even if, yes, you do it for the least bad of intentions.. you're still choosing to use a tool that inherently is bad, when you always have other options.
It's not like this is the only option and it's a life-saving reason and the ethics weigh in your favor.. you could choose to put lorem ipsum.. you could choose to put a sketch or a placeholder doodle.
You chose to use a tool that stole from artists and writers and humans and that only feeds big corp and big money, and smooths out our collective experience to the lowest common denominator, giving us slop.
You could've done anything else.. this wasn't necessary.
