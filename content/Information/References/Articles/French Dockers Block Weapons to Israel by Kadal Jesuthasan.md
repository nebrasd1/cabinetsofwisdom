### id901377682
> This isn’t the first time that dockers in Fos, an industrial area in the south of France, have taken a stand against weapons shipments to Israel. [In April](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=981668934166096&set=a.559041409762186), after learning about a ship allegedly carrying [F-35 parts](http://ae) to Israel, they threatened to stop work. The company, Maersk, agreed to let them examine the containers, which turned up clean. The ship was delayed by several days, but a week later, Maersk [discretely removed](https://www.maersk.com/news/articles/2025/04/22/sln-service-mediterranean-sea-c) Marseille from its route to Haifa, assuring its clients that it sought to “keep your supply chain moving with as little disruption as possible.”
