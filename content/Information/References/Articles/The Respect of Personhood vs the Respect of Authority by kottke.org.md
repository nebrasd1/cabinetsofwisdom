### id978647300
> In April 2015, [Autistic Abby wrote on their Tumblr](https://web.archive.org/web/20150404010354/http://stimmyabby.tumblr.com/post/115216522824/sometimes-people-use-respect-to-mean-treating) about how people mistakenly conflate two distinct definitions of “respect” when relating to and communicating with others.
> > Sometimes people use “respect” to mean “treating someone like a person” and sometimes they use “respect” to mean “treating someone like an authority”
> > 
> > and sometimes people who are used to being treated like an authority say “if you won’t respect me I won’t respect you” and they mean “if you won’t treat me like an authority I won’t treat you like a person”
> > 
> > and they think they’re being fair but they aren’t, and it’s not okay.
