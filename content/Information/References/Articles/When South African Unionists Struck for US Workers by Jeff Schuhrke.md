### id894713315

> In 1986, black workers in apartheid South Africa walked off the job in support of unionists in New Jersey. Their strike marked a rare moment of international labor solidarity at the height of deindustrialization and apartheid.

🔗 [View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jw65jyb41erct3zfc9ayh4rk)

### id894713345

> in the 1980s, when factory closures were in full swing. At the time, many US unions strongly supported the [Free South Africa Movement](https://africanactivist.msu.edu/organization/210-813-110/) by [boycotting](https://africanactivist.msu.edu/recordFiles/210-849-26271/boycottshellguideopt.pdf) multinational corporations that did business with the apartheid government, like Shell Oil; [refusing](https://workdaymagazine.org/this-union-is-famous-for-opposing-south-african-apartheid-now-its-standing-with-gaza/) to handle South African [cargo](https://jacobin.com/2024/11/ilwu-apartheid-south-africa-boycott); and [divesting](https://www.nytimes.com/1984/08/04/nyregion/pension-system-to-drop-stocks-over-apartheid.html) their pension funds from companies linked to the country.

🔗 [View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jw65ksj21w0n4pa4vj9y9b80)

### id894713351

> this solidarity went both ways — particularly when hundreds of South African workers courageously staged a brief but powerful sympathy strike to protest a plant closing in the United States in support of New Jersey workers facing layoffs.

🔗 [View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jw65m13fzsdw8scvkd9jzxk9)

### id894713574

> after learning of the struggle of their fellow unionists in New Jersey, on February 28, the entire black workforce at the plant staged an [afternoon walkout](https://americanarchive.org/catalog/cpb-aacip-507-7m03x8472q?start=2885.62\&end=3423.95) to protest the layoffs in Freehold, temporarily halting production. Marching and dancing out of the building, they sported T-shirts reading: “Don’t Abandon Freehold, My Hometown” — the same shirts regularly worn by OCAW Local 8-760 members in New Jersey.
