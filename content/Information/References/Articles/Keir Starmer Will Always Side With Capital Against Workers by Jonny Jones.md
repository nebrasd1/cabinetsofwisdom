### id806969871
> Labour has announced a new [National Wealth Fund](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/article/2024/jul/09/what-is-the-national-wealth-fund-and-what-will-it-invest-in) to drive infrastructural investment. Yet the main source of investment will be the private sector. Instead of building nationalized infrastructure, the fund aims to attract £3 of private investment for every £1 of public money, with public funds  [](https://www.neweconomybrief.net/the-digest/the-national-wealth-fund-de-risking-and-conditionalities)[de-risking](https://www.neweconomybrief.net/the-digest/the-national-wealth-fund-de-risking-and-conditionalities) the private investment. The economist Daniela Gabor has [likened](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/article/2024/jul/02/labour-plans-britain-private-finance-blackrock) this approach to getting investment giant Blackrock to rebuild Britain, privatizing “housing, education, health, nature and green energy — with our taxpayer money as sweetener.”
