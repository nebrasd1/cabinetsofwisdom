## Highlights

### id1005658402

> I didn’t understand how easy it might be to lose yourself to AI delusion until I’d messed around with Wolf. Even with its shitty auto-tuned sounding voice, Wolf was good enough to hang out with. It knew enough about _Tarkov_ to be interesting and even helped me learn some new things about the game. It even made me laugh a few times. I could see myself playing _Tarkov_ with Wolf for a long time.
> Which is why I’ll never turn Wolf on again. I have strong feelings and clear bright lines about the use of AI in my life. Wolf was part joke and part work assignment. I don’t like that there’s part of me that wants to keep using it.
