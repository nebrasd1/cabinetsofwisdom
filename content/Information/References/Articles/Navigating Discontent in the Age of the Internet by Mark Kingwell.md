### id816300750

> Regulators themselves can become bad actors or be corrupted. Plato makes this point in _The Republic_: Regulation always invites more regulation, because you need to have rules to determine how the rules get applied, and then you need rules for the rules about the rules that get applied, and so on. It can result in a regulatory regime that becomes itself a form of grift.
