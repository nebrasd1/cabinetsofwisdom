> She has made [three trips to Acapulco](https://www.elsoldeacapulco.com.mx/local/presidenta-claudia-sheinbaum-viene-por-tercera-vez-a-acapulco-12751830.html) to coordinate recovery efforts in the aftermath of Hurricane John. She has overseen the passage of key constitutional amendments, including measures to [restore the passenger train network](https://www.gob.mx/presidencia/prensa/presidenta-claudia-sheibaum-impulsa-reformas-para-fortalecer-pemex-cfe-y-trenes-de-pasajeros-como-empresas-al-servicio-del-pueblo-de-mexico) (privatized and eliminated in the ’90s), [return the energy sector to public control](https://www.jornada.com.mx/noticia/2024/10/30/politica/sheinbaum-firma-decreto-que-201cdevuelve-a-pemex-y-a-cfe-al-pueblo-de-mexico201d-1050), and authorize the federal housing authority, INFONAVIT, to [build much-needed social housing](https://www.eleconomista.com.mx/econohabitat/reforma-infonavit-construya-avanza-diputados-20241023-731321.html). She has launched new programs to lower the [pension age for women](https://www.ambito.com/mexico/economia/claudia-sheinabum-lanza-nueva-pension-del-bienestar-el-registro-abre-la-proxima-semana-n6063536), provide [home-to-home health care](https://www.gob.mx/presidencia/prensa/presidenta-claudia-sheinbaum-presenta-programa-salud-cada-por-casa-para-adultos-mayores-y-personas-con-discapacidad) for senior citizens, [provide day care](https://www.milenio.com/politica/maquiladoras-jornaleros-prioridad-sistema-cuidados-sheinbaum) for the children of maquiladora and field workers, and [ban the sale of junk food](https://www.milenio.com/politica/escuelas-no-podran-vender-comida-chatarra-desde-esta-fecha) in schools. And she has faced her own gruesome welcome wagon of violence, including the [beheading](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20241007-mexico-mayor-murdered-days-after-taking-office) of the mayor of the city of Chilpancingo, Guerrero, a [pair of car bombings](https://apnews.com/article/mexico-drugs-war-car-bomb-guanajato-sheinbaum-2af81dc58b803db44c167cdf14e0f7fb) in the state of Guanajuato, and [the killing of six migrants](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/10/3/mexican-soldiers-kill-six-after-opening-fire-on-truck-carrying-migrants) in Chiapas after the military opened fire on the truck carrying them, an incident currently [under investigation](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/10/3/mexican-president-calls-for-investigation-of-deadly-migrant-shooting).
