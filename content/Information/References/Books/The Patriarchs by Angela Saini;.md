## Document Notes
The fear about the idea of women being in power was likely rooted in the belief that female freedom would threaten the integrity of the state. This belief was likely compounded by the fact that in many societies, women were viewed as naturally weaker and in need of protection, and male kings were common in ancient societies. Additionally, there was a fear that women may revolt if they were not kept under control, which was likely heightened by the presence of foreign captives in some societies.
