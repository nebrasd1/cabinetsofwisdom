# Recommendations
## Steam Deck Games
- **Animal Well** - If you don't know this game, don't look it up. Just play and revel in this very absorbing metroidvania platformer. The aspect of all the secrets in this game is its strongest feature.
- **Balatro** - Just do it.
- **Citizen Sleeper** - One of my favorite games ever. Excellent narrative and writing, fun gameplay using dice, just a good time all around.
- **Case of the Golden Idol** - Excellent puzzle game ala Return of the Obra Dinn.
- **Celeste** - A classic of a platformer  that is always the right answer to play.
- **Chants of Sennaai** - Puzzle type game where you are deciphering alien languages.. very meditative and makes your brain think gooder.
- **Cobalt Core** - Roguelike deckbuilder in space, think FTL or Into the Breach or Slay the Spire.
- **Dave the Diver** - I didn't like this that much, but purely because I could see thru the matrix of how it was making me get addicted to the gameplay loops. It's very good at what it does. (Didn't like all the fat-shaming either)
- **Dome Keeper** - One of those mining games that are always so addictively fun, combined with a clever tower-defense kinda thing with your base up top. 
- **Dredge** - Fishing game mixed with Lovecraftian theme
- **Hades**
- **Hades 2**
- **Hi-Fi Rush** - If you want a rhythm-based combat game with a fun story and bopping music, this is it.
- **Minishoot** - Okay, this is an insane pitch: It's Legend of Zelda, but you're a spaceship. You're still on the ground, this isn't in space. It's very much Zelda-style map and gameplay with all the trappings of bosses and powers that help unlock other areas etc. and it's such a treat.
- **Shogun Showdown** - tactical turn-based roguelike where your position matters most. Excellent game. 
- **SteamWorld Heist 2** - I love the SteamWorld universe and this is a well done tactics game within it
- **Tactical Breach Wizards** - Tactics game ala xcom, fun challenges, insane abilities, with awesome and funny writing.
