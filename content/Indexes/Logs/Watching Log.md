- Don't Look Up
	- A movie where the system is the villain
	- I can't believe I didn't watch this right away and fell for the media critique and lambasting of it.
	- It's too real, and too scary, and too bonkers.
	- The throughline of science as a tool; of needing it when desired and discarding it when not.
	- Perfectly primed twist of saving the comet for capitalistic reasons (that are presented, as always, as the answer to poverty, hunger, and eternal human growth).
	- Oglethorpe.. I mean, correct me if I'm wrong, but seems to be one of the only solid guys throughout. Level-headed, in the know, good bullshit detector, and just really on point.
	- Chris Evans's character, as brief as that cameo is, is one of the most insidiously/subtly worst. He argues for stopping the politics, not having a side, stop the virtue-signaling, and says both sides are valid.. look up AND don't look up. It's insane and yet something I probably would've fallen for in the past. This is isn't a situation where both sides are valid.. think if you were trying to apply that to earth is round/earth is flat.
		- Yes, it gets more complex when it's something we can't see, or when it's something non-scientific, like humanity, but that's what the first half of the movie covers too really.
	- The presentation of the movie is pitch perfect. The social media of it all, the factions, the types of people, the media, the ones that deny, the ones that take advantage, etc.
	- Jonah Hill as chief of staff mama's boy is "chef's kiss". 
		- "I want to give a prayer for stuff. For all the sick stuff that could go away and I don't wanna see that stuff go away." Just, my god.. wow. 
	- The president forgetting to bring her son on the escape craft. Fantastic.
- Sonic 3
	- Awesome entry. Everyone is having a blast doing this movie. I mean, Jim Carrey, Idris Elba and Keannu Reeves? In a Sonic movie? Wild. The key, as always, is to never take yourself too seriously and just have fun.
	- I never would've guessed my favorite part would be Jim Carrey dancing with himself to a bopper of a tune.
	- And always grateful for seeing Stone and his relationship. "Not sycophant, but sycofriend."
- Creature Commandos
	- Not a fan. I think this brand of comedy/violence, ala Suicide Squad and so on, is not my cup of tea. I get that they went and made every character full of pathos, for a bunch of killers and monsters, but still.. just violent for violence's sake, and nothing meaningful happening.
- Shrinking Season 2
	- Excellent show with a great cast of characters that play well off of each other.
- Silo Seasons 1 and 2
	- Damn, this hooks you right away with the setting, the mysteries, etc.
	- I'll admit, I was much more attached to all the characters they killed off early on.
	- Season 2 is feeling a bit weaker and suffering from the usual sequel-itis of making things BIGGER. Juliette is sidelined on the world's most inane set of sidequests that have no bearing on things whatsoever, but I suppose it's cool to see a rebellion being grown. What's less encouraging is having the full awareness that the rebellion is pointless in the sense that, as soon as they get their way, they all die.

# Shows I Watched
* 2023-08-08: [[Star Trek - Strange New Worlds (2022–)]] S02E09 - That thought, about two opposite things being true at the same time, just popped up again and opened up a can of worms in my mind.
	* Chapel is singing about being happy for all her hard work being paid off and joining a fellowship, but this comes at the expense of her relationship with Spock.
		* We definitely need to be celebrating and happy for Chapel. The age-old response here would be that we need to be sad for Spock, because he's the man and he's being heartbroken due to a woman following her bliss instead of him.
		* Guess what, we can just feel both, man. Happy for her, sad for him. Neither thing has to overpower the other, contradict it, or worst of all, negate it. We can be sad for him, but that shouldn't mean she can't be or doesn't deserve to be happy and follow her dream. We can be happy for her, but that doesn't mean we don't have some empathy for his loss as well.
		* Freaking both opposite things can exist in the same space and both be true and valid! So much of toxic behavior comes from not being able to hold both thoughts for some reason, like our brains and hearts are so small that they can only contain one singular thought? Seriously?
		* Spock's breakup depression song consisting of math puns is amazing (I'm the ex / x; I solve for why / y). So so good.
		* Captain Pike's falling to his knees moment
		* Referencing Buffy's Once More with Feeling when they talked about being turned into bunnies.
	* Klingons performing K-Pop is (chef's kiss) a masterpiece of synergy. Honestly, they couldn't and shouldn't perform in any other way.
* 2023-08-07: [[Star Trek - Strange New Worlds (2022–)]] S02E08 - War episode about reformed Klingon ambassador. Pike and M'Benga had a conversation at the end that brings up a good question.
	* Pike says the Federation believes everyone deserves a second change. M’Benga asks about justice and the victims. “Doesn’t everyone deserve to pay for their actions?” Pike asks who decides who pays and who gets redemption, and asks where the line is. M'Benga feels that each side of a conflict will differ on that, and when asking about that line, it depends on which side you're viewing it from.
