- Communities
	- It's been bred out of us. We don't look at family and community as anything but an obligation. We don't have a strong sense of solidarity. That requires things like pandemics and crises to clear the fog of systems above us.
- Scale... back to my thing about anything bigger is harder to manage
	- content moderation at scale impossible
	- caring about things at scale impossible
	- That's why i like spider-man.. it's about making one small place better. He's a community hero.
- the common excuse of not doing something because it doesn't fix everywhere... that's not an excuse. Make here better now.. 
- Remembering the lessons from Dispossessed
	- Individuals must always have the right to question, to not work, to have autonomy
	- We always fear that people are selfish, or lazy, or not contributing to greater good, or will take advantage or hurt or so on... but at base level, we won't be like that. When we know the community will take care of us, when people work together, when there's trust and safety, we operate in good will as well. I won't kill or steal or hurt for no reason.
- Unions and communities are about solidarity and helping each other, especially when it's hard, especially when I have a lot or when I have little, because we know everyone else will help me just as I'm helping them. There has to be trust and a sense of community.
