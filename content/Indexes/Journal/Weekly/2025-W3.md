# Reading
- [Gall's Law](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gall_(author)#Gall's_law)
	- *A complex system that works is invariably found to have evolved from a simple system that worked. A complex system designed from scratch never works and cannot be patched up to make it work. You have to start over with a working simple system.*
		- When it comes to keeping your system simple, you have to audit/question whenever you want to make things more complex (even if your excuse is automation, etc.)
			- What's the objective? And how do you manage it?
			- if you're spending more on the process than the input/output, then it's a waste of time.
			- The process needs to have tasks and a regular review. Review is important, to know if things are working, not working, what needs to change, etc.
